# Production Crew Members
## ATL Crew
- Producers, Director, DoP, Cast, Writers
	- Responsible for establishing the film's creative direction, securing financial resources, and making major decisions that shape the project.
		- **Executive Producer**
			- In charge for the financing of the film and therefore the one in charge of the entire production.
		- **Producers**
			- Oversees the film production. Manages the moving parts in the production process. May not be on set.
			- Project Development, Budget management, Hiring, Production Planning and Supervision, Post-Production Oversight, Project Completion
		- **Line Producer**
			- Person responsible for daily operations of the film. Always available on set. In post production they manage deadlines and make sure the budget is on track.
			- Budget management, scheduling, crew management, resource allocation, logistics, cost control, production supervision
		- **Director**
			- Responsible for translating the screenplay into a visual and auditory experience. They lead the creative direction of the film, guiding the performances, cinematography, design, sound, and editing to ensure that all elements work together to communicate the intended story.
				- **Types of Directors**
					- **Director-For-Hire**
						- Hired by a production company/studio/producer to direct a specific film project. They are expected to execute the project's established creative direction while contributing their own interpretation and expertise.
					- **Auteur**

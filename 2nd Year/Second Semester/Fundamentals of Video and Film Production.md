# Production Crew Members
## ATL Crew
- Producers, Director, DoP, Cast, Writers
	- Responsible for establishing the film's creative direction, securing financial resources, and making major decisions that shape the project.
		- **Executive Producer**
			- In charge for the financing of the film and therefore the one in charge of the entire production.
		- **Producers**
			- Oversees the film production. Manages the moving parts in the production process. May not be on set.
			- Project Development, Budget management, Hiring, Production Planning and Supervision, Post-Production Oversight, Project Completion
		- **Line Producer**
			- Person responsible for daily operations of the film. Always available on set. In post production they manage deadlines and make sure the budget is on track.
			- Budget management, scheduling, crew management, resource allocation, logistics, cost control, production supervision
		- **Director**
			- Responsible for translating the screenplay into a visual and auditory experience. They lead the creative direction of the film, guiding the performances, cinematography, design, sound, and editing to ensure that all elements work together to communicate the intended story.
				- **Types of Directors**
					- **Director-For-Hire**
						- Hired by a production company/studio/producer to direct a specific film project. They are expected to execute the project's established creative direction while contributing their own interpretation and expertise.
					- **Auteur Director**
						- A filmmaker whose personal artistic vision, themes, and visual style are strongly reflected in their films. The term auteur comes from the French word for "author", emphasizing the director's role as a major creative force behind a film.
					- **Hybrid Director**
					- **Director of Photography/Cinematographer**
						- Responsible for the overall photographic and visual appearance of a film.
						- Translates film's creative vision into images through decisions of where to point the fucking camera
						- lighting exposure movement and lenses too i guess
					- **Screenwriter**
						- responsible for writing the screenplay
					- **Talent/Cast**
						- **Principal Cast**
							- Central Characters of the film.
							- Lead, Supporting, and Co-Lead Actor/Actress
						- **Minor Supporting Cast**
						- **Bit Players**
							- Performers who have very brief roles.
						- **Extras (Background Actors)**
						- **Stunt Performers**
						- **Voice Actors**
						- **Stand-Ins and Body Doubles**
### BTL Crew
- Extras, Art Dept, Costume Dept, Production, etc.
- Technical, operational, and administrative people
- a lot of departments
	- **Unit Production Manager**
		- Oversees the day-to-day operations of the production. Works closely with the producer and other department heads to keep things organized.
	- **1st AD**
		- Oversees day-to-day filming schedule and manages on-set operations. Coordinates with the director, producer, and department heads to ensure that scenes are filmed efficiently and according to schedule. 
	- **2nd AD**
		- Assists the 1st AD.
	- **Production Assistants**
	- **Continuity Department**
	- **Script Supervisor**
	- **Camera Department**
		- **CamOp**
		- **1st AC/Focus Puller**
		- **2nd AC (Slate)**
## Post-Production Crew
- Editors, Colorists, etc.

# Philosophy
- Philo (love) Sophia (wisdom)
	- Love of wisdom
- Study of fundamental questions about life, knowledge, reality, truth, mortality, and human existence
- Philosophy encourages people to think critically, reason logically, and seek evidence before forming questions.
## Historical Context
- Began more than 2,500 years ago in Ancient Greece
- Before philosophy developed, people explained natural events & human existence through myths, legends, and religious beliefs

- The world today is a neo-liberal/free market
	- The free exchange of goods/services with little tariffs and/or tax impositions by governments
	- This global economy is run by the government and controlled by multi-national/trans-national corporations
		- Companies that have headquarters in an origin country but have a further reach in other countries

## Global Economy
- The network of economic activities that connects countries through the exchange of goods, services, capital, technology and labor
	- Interdependence
	- International Trade
	- Global Investment
	- Global Financial Markets
	- Technology and Connectivity
	- Global Financial Markets
### Importance
- Promotes Economic growth by expanding markets
- Creates employment through international business
- Encourages innovation by sharing tech and knowledge
- Strengthens economies around countries
- Provides consumers with a plethora of goods and services around the globe
### Challenges
- Unequal distribution of wealth
- Economic crises can spread quickly from one country to another
- Dependence on global supply chains
- Environmental impacts 
- Trade disputes can affect global stability

## Trade
- The action of buying or selling goods and services between two parties
- The global exchange of goods, services, capital and data across international borders

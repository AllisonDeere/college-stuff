- The interaction of people, states, countries, and corporations through the exchange of information, ideas, money and culture
- The interconnectedness of people and businesses across the world leading to political, cultural, and economic integration
- We experience globalization every day

## Different Authors' Definition of Globalization
### Anthony Giddens
- What happens in one part of the world, happens in another
	- i.e., War, oil, technology, pop culture, global trends

### Roland Robertson
- Globalization makes the world feel smaller and more connected 
- The world is shrinking because of media and technology, making us more aware that we are part of one global community
	- i.e., messaging apps, global disasters (covid)

### Manfred Steger
- Globalization is a set of processes that increases connections between people, places and ideas across the world
	- i.e., online shopping, use of software, challenges caused by climate change

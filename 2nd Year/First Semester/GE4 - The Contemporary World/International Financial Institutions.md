## International Monetary Fund
- Washington DC
- 191 Countries
	- Short-term loans for countries prevent bankruptcy
		- **Obligations**
			- Accept periodic surveillance of the domestic monetary position and to abide by any conditions attached to a borrowing arrangement
		- **Enforcement**
			- Loans are disbursed in stages/future stages (as well as future loans) are, in theory, dependent on compliance with past conditions
		- **Governed By**
			- Board of Governors (Finance ministers of the member-states) who address policy questions
		- **Senior Group Delegates**
			- Directions of the Executive Boards (24 members includes permanent seats for eight large quota holders) who decides who can get loans and how much
## World Bank
- Washington DC
- 189 members
	- To reconstruct Europe after WW2, now to reduce poverty by lending the money of the rich to the poor countries for specific development projects and by providing technical assistance to poor countries

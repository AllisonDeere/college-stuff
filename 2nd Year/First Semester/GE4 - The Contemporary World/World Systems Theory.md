Creadted by Wallerstein
- Conceptualized from the post-colonial/marxist perspective
- The global economy is based on hierarchy
	- Ergo, there is *no such thing as equality*
		- Power differences are everywhere no matter what
- World is divided into three categories, instead of first and third world
	- Classified based on capacity for capital and labor/division of labor
### Core
- Rich countries, capable military, and independent without the need of economical support from other countries
	- Colonizers
	- e.g., US, Japan, Britian
### Semi Periphery
- Powerful countries that want to become core countries
	- e.g., Brazil, Russia
### Periphery
- Some countries are rich but with developmental constraints
	- i.e., corruption, institutional/systemic issues
- Financially dependent countries
	- e.g., ASEAN countries (except singapore because fuck singapore)


Periphery supplies raw materials -> semi-periphery makes finished product -> core countries sell products back to periphery and semi-periphery with a bigger price

Composition is the arrangement and organization of visual elements within a frame or space
- The foundation of visual communication
# Importance of Composition
- Guides the viewer's eye and attention.
- Enhances storytelling, mood, and emotional impact
- Helps achieve harmony n shit

# Focal Point
- Main area of the image that brings people's eyes towards it.

# Composition Techniques
## Simplicity
- Simplicity in photography is the practie of including what is only essential. Requires the artist to intentionally remove distractions so the subject stands out clearly.

# Rule of Thirds
- This divides an image into 9 equal parts using two horizontal and two vertical lines, creating four key intersection points.
- Placing important elements along these points or aligning horizons with the top or bottom lines.
# Golden Triangle
- compositional technique that balances focal points using diagonals. 
- Unlike the rule of thirds, which emphasizes left right balance, the golden triangle creates harmony across both the top and bottom of the frame as well.

# Lines
- Are a powerful way to lead the viewer's eye towards a focal point

# Shapes
- Shapes are everywhere- whether in the rigid lines of architecture or the organic forms of nature. 

# Frame Within a Frame
- Uses elements within the scene to enclose a subject inside another shape, or 'frame'.
# Textures
- adds a tactile quality to composition, appealing to our sense of touch and enriching the image

# Patterns
- Patterns naturally attract the human eye.

# Space
- Artists must decide how to use this *real estate*.
- Negative and positive space work together.

# Fill The Frame
- filling the frame is a technique where positive space dominates the entire image, leaving little to no negative space.
- Often achieved through cropping, zoom/macro lenses, or extreme close-ups in cinema.
- By focusing solely on the subject, the method demands the viewers attention and when used on faces can create a strong emotional connection between audience and subject.

# Depth
- Depth is having foreground and background. In composition, percieveing depth is by using the foreground for important details and the background for lesser iportant details,

# Balance
- Symmetry.
- The safest way to take photos.
## Asymmetrical balance
- Doesn't mean 'imbalanced'. But the opposite of symmetry.

# Reader Persona Framework
- "Audience Persona" or "User Persona"
	- In Marketing, it is also referred to as "**Buyer Persona**".
	- The psychological identity of an audience.
	- Semi-fictional profile of an ideal customer, built using real data and research to guide marketing. -Adobe
# Layers of a Reader Persona
## Demographics
- The statistical characteristics of a human population. Measurable data points
## Psychographics
- Refers to the qualitative study of consumers based on their psychological attributes focusing on values beliefs interests personalities and lifestyles.
	- Unlike demographics that describe the *who*, psychographics explains the *why* by uncovering the emotional and cognitive drivers behind their behaviour.
		- Opinions on social issues, their hobbies, their daily routines, and their ultimate life goals.
## "Pain Points"
- Pain points are specific persistent problems or frustrations that current or prospective customers face in the marketplace. These challenges can occur at any stage of the customer journey and typically fall into categories like financial (too expensive), productivity (too time consuming), process (too complicated) or support (poor customer service).
- Businesses actively research and identify these friction points to design products, services, or marketing strategies that directly resolve them.
- By positioning a brand as the ultimate solution to a customer's specific struggle, a company can eliminate barriers to purchase and build strong brand loyalty.

# Creating a Persona
## 1: Audit the Digital Habitat (Platform)
- Determine your platform, and think common Reddit user vs. Instagram user.
## 2. Practice "voice observation"
- Determine how users communicate.
## 3. Characterize the "Why".
- Define them as a character with goals. Why do they watch or consume, or write content on that platform?
## 4. Filter.
- Before finalizing anything, test it against your person.
	- *Does it provide value to them?*
	- Use the persona as a filter to keep your creative energy focused on work that resonates, rather than content that satisfies personal purposes.

# Evolution of Media (Timeline)
- Modern media is *ubiquitous* part of modern life.
	- From the moment we wake up to the moment we rest, we **consume**.
	- Integrated so well into our daily lives that we don't need to think before we use media.
- Milleniuals/Gen Y (1981-1996) Represents the first wave of digital natives, from dial-up internet to early smartphones.
- Gen Z (1997-2012) The true mobile digitial natives, growing up with high-speed internet, apps, and social media.
- Gen Alpha(2013-2025) These individuals have interacted with tablets

# Evolution of Media (Timeline)
- Modern media is *ubiquitous* part of modern life.
	- From the moment we wake up to the moment we rest, we **consume**.
	- Integrated so well into our daily lives that we don't need to think before we use media.
- Milleniuals/Gen Y (1981-1996) Represents the first wave of digital natives, from dial-up internet to early smartphones.
- Gen Z (1997-2012) The true mobile digitial natives, growing up with high-speed internet, apps, and social media.
- Gen Alpha(2013-2025) These individuals have interacted with tablets, voice assistants, and AI since infancy.
## The Clay Token
- ~8500BC clay tokens were used to record trades and transactions.
- A tiny clay token meant a small measure of grain,  while a cylinder represented an animal.
	- These primitive, abstract symbols were the early genesis of writing.
## Pre-Industrial Age
- Painting on cave walls, pressing cuneiform into mesopotamian clay tablets, and writing on Egyptian papyrus plants.
- Around 2000 BC, a phonetic system emerged in Egypt that would be the "great-grandmother" of the Roman alphabet we use today (*Proto-Sinaitic script*)
- *Non-fixity*
## Industrial Age
- The Gutenberg Printing press 
- The power of steam and machines allowed the mass production of books and newspapers
	- This area deeply cemented *linearity*, training human brains to digest stories sequentially, front to back, and in the West, from left to right.
- *Fixity*

## Development of Filipino Christian Faith
### Background
Before Christianity arrived, Filipinos practiced indigenous religions. Many communities believed in a supreme creator, worshipped nature spirits, and honored their ancestors. Religious leaders called **babaylan** served as healers and spiritual guides.
### Arrival of Christianity
- Christianity was introduced in **1521** when Ferdinand Magellan arrived in the Philippines during his expedition under Spain.
- The first recorded Christian baptism took place on **March 31, 1521**, on Limasawa.
- Local rulers, including Rajah Humabon and his wife, accepted baptism along with hundreds of their followers.

### Spread of Christianity
- Permanent Spanish colonization began in **1565** under Miguel López de Legazpi.
- Spanish missionaries built churches, schools, hospitals, and towns centered around the parish.
- Religious orders such as the Order of Saint Augustine, Order of Friars Minor, Order of Preachers, Society of Jesus, and Order of Augustinian Recollects established missions across the islands.
- Christianity gradually became part of Filipino culture through fiestas, devotion to saints, and family religious traditions.
### Development
- Catholic beliefs blended with some local customs and traditions.
- Filipino Christianity became community-centered, emphasizing family, prayer, celebrations, and charity.
- Today, the Philippines is one of the world's largest Christian nations, with Christianity remaining a major influence on culture, education, politics, and national identity.

## History of Catholicism
### Catholicism
- Introduced by Spanish explorers and missionaries beginning in **1521**.
- Expanded during over **300 years of Spanish colonial rule (1565–1898)**.
- Missionaries established churches, schools, convents, and hospitals throughout the country.
- Catholicism became deeply connected with Filipino customs, education, and government.
#### Major Contributions
- Spread literacy and education.
- Founded many schools and universities.
- Built historic churches.
- Introduced religious festivals, processions, and devotion to saints.
- Influenced Filipino values such as faith, family unity, and compassion.
## History of Protestantism
### Protestantism
- Introduced after the Spanish–American War.
- Following the Treaty of Paris, Spain ceded the Philippines to the United States.
- American missionaries arrived in **1899** and established Protestant churches.
- Different Protestant denominations agreed to divide missionary work into different regions to reduce competition.
### Major Protestant Denominations
- United Methodist Church
- Convention of Philippine Baptist Churches
- Episcopal Church in the Philippines
- United Church of Christ in the Philippines
- Philippine Independent Church (Although independent from Rome, it developed within the broader Christian tradition and has historical links to Catholic worship.)
### Contributions of Protestantism
- Promoted Bible reading and personal faith.
- Established schools, colleges, hospitals, and social welfare programs.
- Encouraged community service and missionary work.
- Contributed to religious diversity and freedom in the Philippines.

## Development of Filipino Christian Faith
### Background
Before Christianity arrived, Filipinos practiced indigenous religions. Many communities believed in a supreme creator, worshipped nature spirits, and honored their ancestors. Religious leaders called **babaylan** served as healers and spiritual guides.
### Arrival of Christianity
- Christianity was introduced in **1521** when Ferdinand Magellan arrived in the Philippines during his expedition under Spain.
- The first recorded Christian baptism took place on **March 31, 1521**, on Limasawa.
- Local rulers, including Rajah Humabon and his wife, accepted baptism along with hundreds of their followers.

### Spread of Christianity
- Permanent Spanish colonization began in **1565** under Miguel López de Legazpi.
- Spanish missionaries built churches, schools, hospitals, and towns centered around the parish.
- Religious orders such as the Order of Saint Augustine, Order of Friars Minor, Order of Preachers, Society of Jesus, and Order of Augustinian Recollects established missions across the islands.
- Christianity gradually became part of Filipino culture through fiestas, devotion to saints, and family religious traditions.

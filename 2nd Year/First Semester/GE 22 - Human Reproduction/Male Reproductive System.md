# Signs of Puberty in Boys
## Mood Swings 
- Happens early as a child's body adapts to the surge of hormones.
## Growth Spurt
- Feet and hands will suddenly grow bigger
- Height also grows
## Body Odor
- BO becomes a thing
## Body Hair
- Pubic and armpit hair + hair on arms and legs starts to darken and thicken
- Eventually face & maybe chest and back
## Genital Changes
- First his testicles grow bigger, then penis grows wider and longer
- Foreskin should be fully retractable by now, between 13-16 years of age ejaculation is possible
## Pimples & Oily Hair
- Oil glands start to work which causes pimples, hair also becomes oily
## Voice Changes
- His voice will start to crack as voice box grows bigger

# Penis
- Male organ used for urination and sexual intercourse. 
- During puberty, the penis lengthens
- Ability to ejaculate begins at 12-14 years old

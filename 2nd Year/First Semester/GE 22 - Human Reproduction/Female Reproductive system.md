# Ovaries
- Two ovaries are attached to each side of the uterus by a ligament
- Oval-shaped, about the size of a large olive
- lie close to the fimbria at the end of the fallopian tubes
- Already filled at birth with egg-containing sacs called **follicles**
	- Turns into ovum
- The fetus has ~6-7 million oocytes (immature eggs)
	- Decreases to about 1-2 million by the time the female is born
	- Oocytes continues to diminish in number over a woman's life, with ~300,000 - 500,000 remaining at puberty
	- Throughout the woman's reproductive years, only about 300-400 oocytes mature and are released during ovulation
# Fallopian Tubes
- Tubes that connect the ovary to the uterus

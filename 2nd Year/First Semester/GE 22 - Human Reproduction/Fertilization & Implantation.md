Fertilization & implantation are the most critical events in the reproductive process. In this process, both egg and sperm are fused together to form a zygote.

# Fertilization
Egg + Sperm = Zygote
- Then gets implanted into the uterus and the development of an organism.
- Takes place in the **fallopian tube**.
- During this process, semen comprising thousands of sperm are inseminated into the female vagina during sex/coitus.
	- Only a few sperm will succeed in reaching the opening of the fallopian tube. The sperms move towards the uterus and reach the opening of the fallopian tube.
- The egg is alive for 12-24 hours after its release from the ovary. If it is not fertilized by this time, it disintegrates and is shed off by the uterine lining.
- The sperm stays alive for about 72 hours. The sperms swim upwards to the fallopian tube to fertilize the egg. The faster the sperm swims, the earlier it reaches the egg.
## Types of Fertilization
### Internal Fertilization
- The fusion of egg and sperm occurs inside the female reproductive tract. (e.g., humans, cows)
### External Fertilization
- The fusion of male and female gametes occurs outside of the organism. (e.g., sea urchins, frogs, etc.)

# Implantation
- When a fertilized egg and prepared uterine lining meets, implantation occurs and marks the start of pregnancy. Without a fertilized egg, there is no implantation and a period starts.
## Symptoms of Implantation
- Spotting
- Vaginal discharge
- Cramps
- Bloating
- Nausea and Vomiting

# Pregnancy
Established when a fertilized egg has been implanted in the wall of a woman's uterus.
- Normal pregnancy is about 9 months.

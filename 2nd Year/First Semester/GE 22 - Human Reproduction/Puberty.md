- Key stage in transition from adulthood to childhood
	- It can be a challenging & confusing time, and knowing what to expect and why these changes happen helps a person feel more in control as they go through it
- Puberty begins between 8-14
	- Tends to happen in females earlier than males
- Puberty lasts through the teenage years
	- someone may be 20 years old by the time all changes take place 

### Hypothalamus 
- Puberty egins at the hypothalamus which starts signaling the rest of the body that it's time to develop adult characteristics
### Pituitary Gland
- Located below the hypothalamus
	- small pea-sized gland
	- part of the endocrine system
	- in charge of making several essential hormones
		- Gonadotropin hormone-releasing hormone (GnRH)
		- Luteinizing hormone (LH)
		- Follicle Stimulating Hormone (FSH)

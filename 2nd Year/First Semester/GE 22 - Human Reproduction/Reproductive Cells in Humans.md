# Oocytes
Also known as egg cells, they are critical cells in the production of new life and reproduction. Pregnancy may not last sometimes due to the quality of oocytes in a woman's body as she ages. Quality of oocytes decreases as time goes by.

# Ovum
One of the biggest human body cells, measuring about ~100 micrometers in diameter.

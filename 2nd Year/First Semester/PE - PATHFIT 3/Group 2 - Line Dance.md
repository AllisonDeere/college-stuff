# History & Origin
- Line dancing is a choreographed dance style where individuals dance in lines (or rows) performing a series of repeated steps to music. 
- Originated in the [United States in the early 20th century](https://www.studocu.com/ph/document/asian-college-foundation/english-for-academic-and-profesional-purposes/line-dance-summary-english-for-academic-and-profesional-purposes/113862207?sid=ad8ecf65-ba40-4451-bb14-5e5707a1fdef1788226651) and became popular in the 1970s-1980s, particularly within the country and western music scene. 
- Influenced by traditional folk and social dances. 
- Gained international appeal as it adapted to various music genres, including pop, rock, and Latin.
- Today it's practiced worldwide in clubs, schools, and social gatherings.

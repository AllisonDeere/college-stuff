# History & Origin
- [Line dancing]([https://eodance.ca/articles/art_what_is_line_dance.php](https://eodance.ca/articles/art_what_is_line_dance.php)) is a choreographed dance style where individuals dance in lines (or rows) performing a series of repeated steps to music. 
- Originated in the [United States in the early 20th century](https://www.studocu.com/ph/document/asian-college-foundation/english-for-academic-and-profesional-purposes/line-dance-summary-english-for-academic-and-profesional-purposes/113862207?sid=ad8ecf65-ba40-4451-bb14-5e5707a1fdef1788226651) and became popular in the 1970s-1980s, particularly within the country and western music scene. 
- Influenced by traditional folk and social dances. 
- Gained international appeal as it adapted to various music genres, including pop, rock, and Latin.
- Today it's practiced worldwide in clubs, schools, and social gatherings.
- **very lola/lolo core**
# Characteristics
1. [**No Physical Contact Between Dancers](https://dancewithmeusa.com/what-is-line-dancing/)** (Partnerless Syngergy)
	- Line dancing eliminates the "lead and follow" friction found in other styles. Anyone can join the line immediately and dance independently.
2. **"Walls"**
	- Line dances are structured around a set number of directions you face during the routine.
	- A **1-Wall** dance means you face the same direction the entire time, **2-Wall** dance has you rotate 180º to face the front & back, and a **4-Wall** dance turns you 90º after each sequence .
3. **Rhythm**
	- Routines are broken down by 16, 32 or 64 counts which resets seamlessly once the count sequence finishes, and loops until the song ends.

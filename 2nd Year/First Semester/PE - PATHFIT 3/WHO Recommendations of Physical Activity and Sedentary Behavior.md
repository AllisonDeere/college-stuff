## IPAQ - International Physical Activity Questionnaire
- WHO (2020) issued a comprehensive evidence-based guidelines on physical activity/sedentary behavior that covers all population groups.
- For adolescents/children, 60 minutes a day at least 3 days a week
- For adults, more than 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity and more than 150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic physical activity
- Older adults, 150-300 minutes of moderate-intensity activity per week
